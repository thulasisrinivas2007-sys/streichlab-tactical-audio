## Brand & Style
The design system powers an unconventional, high-energy prank toolkit and audio soundboard. It sits at the intersection of illicit underground tech decks, elite esports consoles, and cyberpunk hacker terminals. The aesthetic blends high-precision technical gear with irreverent, unpredictable audio mayhem.

The visual style unites **dark-mode glassmorphism** with **high-contrast neon cyberpunk cues**:
- Deep obsidian and void-indigo surfaces form an atmospheric base.
- High-frequency neon accentsâ€”electric amber, cyber red, radioactive green, and ultraviolet purpleâ€”signal reactive states, audio triggers, hazard levels, and real-time playback.
- Visual layers incorporate sleek frosted surfaces, subtle edge glows, tactile audio equalizers, and intentional digital glitch artifacts during audio detonation states.
- The UI should feel immediate, tactile, and dangerously fun: equal parts professional laboratory equipment and late-night digital mischief.

## Colors
The palette balances an ultra-deep obsidian foundation with vivid radioactive primaries, alerts, and synthwave accents:

- **Primary (`#FFB800` - Electric Amber):** Main brand voice, primary execution buttons, master volume sliders, active sound triggers, and playback progress indicators.
- **Secondary (`#00FF66` - Radioactive Green):** Armed states, active playback monitors, loop status, healthy latency signals, and dynamic VU meters.
- **Tertiary (`#FF1E56` - Cyber Red):** Instant panic stop, air raid alarms, disruptive prank triggers, self-destruct timers, and overdrive meters.
- **Accent Purple (`#A855F7` - Neon Violet):** Pitch shifters, voice mod filters, custom SFX racks, and specialized prank modules.
- **Surfaces & Neutrals:**
  - Base Void: `#0B0F17` (Deepest canvas background)
  - Surface Substratum: `#131B2E` (Layer 1 panels, channel racks)
  - Surface Elevated: `#1A243B` with 60% opacity for frosted overlays
  - Border Glass: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` and active state neon rim outlines at 40% opacity
  - High-Contrast Text: `#FFFFFF` (Headings, active values), `#94A3B8` (Subheaders, system labels), `#475569` (Muted/inactive nodes)

## Typography
The typographic architecture reinforces the duality of high-speed arcade triggers and technical instrument displays:

- **Headlines (`Space Grotesk`):** Aggressive, geometric, and modern. Conveys weight and presence for pad banks, category titles, and sound engine modes.
- **Body (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Clean, crisp, neutral sans-serif ensuring effortless legibility in low-light and high-stimulus environments.
- **Metadata, Counters & Hotkeys (`JetBrains Mono`):** Monospaced precision for timecodes, decibel indicators, buffer sizes, pad keyboard hotkeys (`[SPACE]`, `[NUM_1]`), and console log feeds. Always renders uppercase for labels and tracking headers.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a modular soundboard grid engineered for sub-second visual scanning and fast tap/click reaction times:

- **Responsive Grid:** 
  - **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column dynamic grid with a persistent left rack (prank toolkit & FX channels) and an expansive sound pad matrix (4x4 or 6x4 configurations).
  - **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with collapsable FX drawer and high-density 3x4 sound pad matrix.
  - **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column flow prioritizing a 2-column or 3-column large touch pad grid, with quick-access swipeable drawer sheets for logs and pitch modulators.
- **Touch-First Hit Targets:** Soundboard trigger pads maintain a strict minimum dimension of 64px on mobile and 80px on desktop to prevent misfires.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is built through layered dark glass, atmospheric backlight diffusion, and luminous neon energy:

- **Layer 0 (Base Canvas):** Solid `#0B0F17` enhanced with a subtle radial gradient mesh (`#131B2E` at 30% center-falloff) and an ultra-fine 1px terminal dot grid.
- **Layer 1 (Control Panels & Decks):** `#131B2E` at 85% opacity, backdrop-filter blur of `16px`, bordered by `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`.
- **Layer 2 (Sound Pads & Interactive Tiles):** Frosted dark slate (`rgba(26, 36, 59, 0.65)`), backdrop-filter blur of `12px`, with an inner highlight bevel (`box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`).
- **Active & Armed Glow States:**
  - **Amber Ignition:** `0 0 24px rgba(255, 184, 0, 0.45)`, border color `#FFB800`.
  - **Radioactive Pulse:** `0 0 20px rgba(0, 255, 102, 0.4)`, border color `#00FF66`.
  - **Detonation/Critical:** `0 0 28px rgba(255, 30, 86, 0.55)`, border color `#FF1E56`.
- **Glitch & Scanline Overlay:** Triggered sound activation briefly shifts chromatic channels (horizontal RGB split 2px) and injects a transient scanline sweep across the active pad.

## Shapes
The shape language relies on precision-cut, tactical industrial geometry. Roundedness level `1` (Soft - `0.25rem` / `4px` base radius) ensures buttons, pads, and meter modules feel like high-grade hardware switches rather than soft consumer toys:

- **Sound Pads:** `0.375rem` (6px) corner radius with chamfered visual styling, maintaining an angular, hardline synth console feel.
- **Pills & Badges:** Monospace status chips use a hard `2px` to `4px` radius to reflect flight-recorder instrumentation.
- **Sliders & EQ Bars:** Segmented or razor-sharp blocks with strict `0px` to `2px` corners.

## Components

### 1. Soundboard Trigger Pads
- **Resting:** Glassmorphism surface (`rgba(26, 36, 59, 0.6)`), 1px border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`. Displays bold sound title (`Space Grotesk`), mono keybind indicator top-right (`[1]`), and audio duration bottom-right.
- **Hover:** Edge illumination transitions to the assigned track category color (Amber, Green, Red, or Violet) with a soft diffuse glow.
- **Triggered / Active:** Shifts to full neon luminescence, radial backdrop glow, scaled down by 2% for instant tactile feedback, accompanied by a dynamic mini waveform equalizer dancing at the base of the pad.

### 2. Equalizers & VU Meter Displays
- Segmented LED-style vertical and horizontal meter bars.
- Inactive segments render at `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`.
- Active levels ramp from `#00FF66` through `#FFB800` into peak clipping `#FF1E56`.

### 3. Master Emergency "Panic" Button
- High-priority Cyber Red (`#FF1E56`) bordered component.
- Pulsing ambient warning glow, diagonal hazard stripes on active hover, instantly halting all active audio playback buffers.
